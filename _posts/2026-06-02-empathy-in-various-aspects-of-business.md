Empathy is a skill that gives one the ability to understand the feelings, circumstances, intentions, thoughts, and needs of others, and to consider them when making decisions.
It is the ability to understand other people and solve problems from their perspective. This article looks at empathy as a business skill and tool in various aspects of business and explores ways to practise empathy in business.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Entrepreneurship
====
Empathy is one of the five key skills of entrepreneurship. It gives entrepreneurs the ability to relate to how others are feeling because they have been in a similar situation themselves.
It is a skill that helps entrepreneurs understand the reality of being an entrepreneur or evaluate their ability to become one.
For example, when an entrepreneur or a potential entrepreneur reads about other entrepreneurs or interviews them, that person is developing empathy.
Such exercises help individuals understand what those entrepreneurs have been through by putting themselves in their shoes.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Design Thinking
====
Empathy is a thinking tool that can be used to create innovative products. Every great product or service is the result of a creative idea. However, before that idea emerged, there was a conscious and deliberate effort to understand the feelings, circumstances, intentions, thoughts, and needs of others.
As one of the essential tools in the design process, empathy helps in understanding the emotional and cultural realities of the people for whom you are designing. Therefore, it enables you to create better solutions that add value and attract customers to your business idea.
Empathy also helps individuals connect with potential collaborators, audiences, and readers in a more meaningful way. For example, empathy as a business tool in content writing is about writing for, and with, the reader in mind.
When empathy is used in the right way, it becomes a business tool that helps identify the unmet needs of others, leading to the creation of new products and services.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Marketing
====
In order to be successful, a business needs to ensure that its marketing programmes are appropriate for its customers. Empathy helps businesses get into the customer's mindset and provides the ability to truly understand the customer better.
This helps a business make accurate assessments of the needs, wants, and thought processes of its target market.
In addition, the empathy skills of those responsible will determine the extent to which information about customer needs and wants is used wisely and honestly to provide products and services that meet those needs.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Management
====
Empathy as a business skill in management refers to a leader's ability to understand the feelings and viewpoints of subordinates and take those perspectives into account when making decisions.
Furthermore, empathy is one of the attributes of emotional intelligence, and skilled leaders use empathy and social skills to earn the loyalty of their subordinates.
Such leaders also tend to be effective at settling disputes between managers and are better able to find common ground and shared purpose among diverse areas of the organisation. This skill is also found among leaders who are able to move people in a desired direction.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Customer Service
====
Empathy in customer service is about putting customers first. It is one of the dimensions of quality service. Every customer wants to be considered and treated as an individual rather than as a number.
Empathy determines the extent and quality of individualised attention an organisation gives its customers.
Empathy helps make great customer service personnel because it enables them to focus relentlessly on the customer.
Without empathy, a customer service officer would be unable to provide excellent customer service. They would be unable to understand customer needs and challenges from the customer's point of view.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Network Relationships
====
In immigrant entrepreneurship, elements of mutual relationships, quality, and ongoing exchanges between business partners and collaborators are often explained using the synonymous terms guanxi and tie or strength of tie.
The former is used in Eastern network literature, while the latter is found in Western network literature.
These concepts describe how network relationships contribute to business success through trust, understanding, support, and the reciprocation of past favours.
Empathy has been one of the four dimensions of guanxi long before Western literature paid significant attention to the concept in business relationships.
Therefore, from this perspective, empathy is a relational business tool. It refers to the ability of customers and sellers to understand each other's needs within network relationships.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Human Resource Management
====
Empathy is important in the development of personal skills and employability. It involves responding to the practical and emotional needs of colleagues.
Organisations expect candidates to demonstrate respect and empathy toward others. In addition to qualities such as pragmatism, decisiveness, and experience, managers are often selected for their empathy.
From an international human resources perspective, adaptability is one of the key criteria used in selecting expatriates who possess the ability to put themselves in others' situations and identify with them.
Cultural empathy is a key trait of adaptability and is one of the selection criteria used in assessing a candidate's potential for success abroad.
International managers are expected to develop empathy and tolerance toward cultural differences. Being able to demonstrate empathy for different viewpoints makes them more positively perceived in the host country.
Empathy enhances an expatriate's interactions with people. It indicates an ability to understand and accept diversity, develop honest friendships with foreign nationals, and show a willingness to use, no matter how rudimentary, the host-country language.

Empathy in Various Aspects of Business – Philanthropy
====
Another aspect of empathy is that it generates genuine concern for the needs of others and the desire to serve them.
It involves experiencing others' emotions by imagining their perspectives and situations, and it is a significant indicator of giving.
Philanthropy is about compassion and giving back. It goes beyond empathy by encouraging action and can be described as the external expression of internal empathy.
It is through empathy that charities and philanthropic initiatives create significant social impact without questionable ulterior motives.

Ways to Practise Empathy
====
Practise Empathy Through Observation
====
Practising empathy through observation involves not only thinking about what people need but also exploring how they behave.

Practise Empathy by Engaging People in Conversation
====
Engaging people in conversation is another way to practise empathy. It involves asking people how they feel and what they think and then genuinely listening to them.

Practise Empathy by Interviewing People
====
Exercises such as interviewing people can help you develop empathy for what they have experienced and what they need. Ask "why" questions to avoid making assumptions. Such questions enable you to put yourself in others' shoes because you gain a clearer understanding of their situation and can imagine what you would do in the same circumstances.

Practise Empathy by Watching and Listening
====
To ensure that your product or service relates to your audience, watch and listen to them carefully.
