Current Courses
======
UIL-MKT 106: The Nigerian Marketing System & Commercial Policy (2 Units C: LH 30)

Course Description: 
This course examines the traditional Nigerian marketing system and explains the concept of a marketing system and how it operates. It covers the meaning and relationship between itinerant, retail, and wholesale trade, as well as the basic concepts of distribution channels. Students will learn the factors to consider when selecting distribution channels, the policies and functions of distribution systems, and the various government regulations that guide commercial activities. The course also highlights the roles of regulatory agencies such as the Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON), the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA), and the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) in ensuring fair and efficient marketing practices.




MKT 220: Food & Agricultural Marketing (2 Units C: LH 30) 

Course Description:
Agricultural marketing covers the services involved in moving an agricultural produce from the farm to the consumer. These services involve the planning, organizing, directing as well as the application of marketing tools in the handling of agricultural produce in such a way as to satisfy farmers, intermediaries and consumers.

MKT 402: Marketing Management (2 Units C: LH 30)

Course Description:
This course focuses on the application of fundamental management principles to marketing activities and decision-making. It examines the organization, planning, control, coordination, and integration of marketing functions within an enterprise. The course explores the elements of the marketing mix, including product development, pricing, promotion, and physical distribution, and their roles in achieving organizational objectives. It also discusses the relationship between marketing and social responsibility, emphasizing ethical business practices, consumer welfare, and the growing importance of consumerism in modern marketing environments.
