In this dataset, I explain the theoretical underpinnings and the process of carrying out EFA (PCA method) on quantitative survey data from the Consultative Group to Assist the Poor (CGAP) Smallholder Household Survey. In the dataset, I provide a step-by-step tutorial on the analysis in IBM SPSS 29.
