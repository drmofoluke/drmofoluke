 Through a longitudinal case study, a microfoundations-based theoretical framework is developed to explain how IAB-EEs develop from the complex interactions between entrepreneurs and the collective actions of local communities, which can, in turn, shape resource mobilisation. The study contributes to research at the intersection of IAB entrepreneurship, transnational diaspora entrepreneurship, and EEs and generates concrete implications for IAB entrepreneurs and ecosystem builders.
